# Drill mechanics

- **Spike Trainer:** leaving the runway parks an unused ball; returning resumes it without clearing action or jump preparation. Without the ball economy, the attempt continues until its timer expires.
- **Salmon Ladder:** I mounts the bar. Hold Space to load a pull. Hold/release left click to crunch and kip, then release Space near the kip peak (about a quarter second later). A full pull with a well-timed full kip succeeds at Core Strength 50. Core Strength (an Endurance subattribute) widens the release window and increases assistance; excellent attempts at high ratings can skip a notch. Rest hanging to recover stamina; I stops. A backswing and knee-to-chest crunch show the kip. Each launch rises above its target and descends onto the support before progression counts; a short launch visibly drops back onto the old notch. Numbered heights, highlighted supports, and clearance feedback show the result. Completion trains Core Strength.
- **Weighted Balancing:** mount the beam and traverse it with weights held at the wrists. Gusts vary in strength and duration. Lateral movement mostly counters the unstable load, with small foot corrections. Stability reduces disturbance; excessive lean or stepping off loses balance.
- **Inclined Obstacles:** the ramp has five footholds joined by corrugated gold regions built into the same surface and collision mesh. Smooth rock cannot support uphill walking or sprinting and forces a downhill slide. Only sprinting along the ridges supplies uphill traction, capped at 1.4–2.2 m/s with Explosiveness; walking on ridges still slips. Ascending multiplies all stamina expenditure by five, including action costs. Recover on level footholds between bursts; touch each in order. The timer allows 60 seconds.
- **Balance Ball Setting:** repeated wall feeds are set into the gold square. The sphere rotates both the athlete's facing and balance; WASD counters lean and partially opposes rotation. Lean over 0.58 radians causes a fall and consumes a ball in economy mode. Stability reduces disturbance. The existing ball/reward system remains in use.

The motor calls optional drill motion and terrain hooks. Animation applies drill poses after ordinary posing, preserving set animation on the balance ball. Terrain expenditure resets to normal each motor frame. Starting a workout clears ordinary preparation; stopping/resetting removes its custom state.
