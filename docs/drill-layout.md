# Narrow drill lanes and jump-serve feed

Spike Trainer occupies a 6 m-wide lane (x -19 to -13). Its suspended ball is standing reach + 1.40 m, 30 cm above the earlier setting. The painted pad extends only from the runway start at z 4.0 through the far edge of the 2.8 m target ring at z −9.7. A 1.5 m-wide floor runway runs from z 4.0 toward the plant stripe at z 1.0, centered at x -16. Start/plant labels and right-left-inward foot marks suggest the approach rhythm. These are provisional guidance, not a movement lock; physique and approach execution still matter.

Serve Receive occupies a 6 × 9 m lane (x 13–19, z 0–9): its near end lines up with the Net and its far end with the home court endline (a full 9 m half court). Feeds land only in the receiving half, in its back part (z 7.0–8.7), so a receive has room to rise before the half line. The six spots come in a shuffled order: each is served once before any is served again, and a spot is never served twice in a row. The participant receives toward the entire opposite half (z 0–4.5): its left half scores 1 point, and the 2-point area is one quadrant of it: x 16–19, z 0–2.25, the far-right corner. A receive must also clear the red holographic barrier along the half line (z 4.5, 3.4 m high, provisional): the ball path's height where it crosses that line is what counts, and under the barrier the receive scores 0 wherever it lands. A receive made from inside the scoring half is judged by its height at contact. Floor colors and the center line mark the regions. Eight points completes the drill and grants the existing completion reward.

Points are awarded only when the received ball lands. Shanks stay in play; wrong-half/outside landings score zero and another serve follows. An untouched serve still fails the attempt. Leaving/reentering the station does not stop a running attempt. A 12-second return safety timeout advances with zero points. Serve patterns and charge demands cycle independently of score, allowing more than six serves. Each feed telegraphs a 600 ms upward toss and an animated overhead paddle strike. Feeds retain a 3.60 m strike height, z -10 origin and 22–25 m/s horizontal pace, but are now solved to actual floor targets inside the far half of the full area. This off-court lane has no net.

bot_facing_drill_layout_test.gd checks approach orientation, airborne commitment, shot limits, lane widths and all six serve trajectories. Map-drill and station-approach tests complete both actual contact sequences. render_drill_layout.gd captures the floor markings and launch location.

Once Serve Receive starts, its painted bounds only designate the entry area. Leaving does not stop the sequence or erase progress, and reentry does not cancel a live feed. The scheduled serves continue; an unreceived ball ends the streak normally. Active drill status remains available outside the lane.
